    How it works

    1. Post in the Telegram bot

      The bot asks what you have, what you want for it, and for a photo. No photo? Use the official catalog one. Your team name and city come from your team’s registration.

    2. Teams find it here

      Every active listing shows on this site. Anyone can search by part, city or team, without an account.

    3. Agree in a private chat

      Tap Message on a listing to open a Telegram chat with that team. You agree on the price and the hand-over between yourselves.
